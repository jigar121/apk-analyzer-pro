How long does a deep scan take?

Our parallelized static engine processes standard APKs in under 15 seconds. Complex enterprise-level apps with high obfuscation may take up to 45 seconds for full trace reconstruction.

Is my APK data kept private?

Privacy is our core tenet. We implement Volatile Memory Processing. Your files never touch a persistent disk and are atomized instantly after the analysis signature is generated.

Do you support App Bundles?

Currently, we focus on .apk standards. Advanced .aab (Android App Bundle) support is in active development and scheduled for our next major Neural engine rollout.

Can I use the API for CI/CD?

Yes! We provide a high-performance RESTful API. Integrate security auditing directly into your Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or GitLab pipelines to ensure zero-vulnerability deployments.
